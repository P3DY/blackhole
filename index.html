Black Hole

You are a black hole adrift among galaxies. Capture stars to grow, swallow galaxy cores and rivals you outweigh, and run from the ones that outweigh you.

A red ring marks a black hole heavier than you: cross it and you won't get back out. Arrows at the screen edge point to black holes out of view, blue for prey and red for danger.

Mouse or WASD drifts · Shift or right mouse button boosts · drag to orbit · scroll to zoom · R restarts · M mutes

Drag to drift · drag past the ring to boost · two fingers orbit and zoom
